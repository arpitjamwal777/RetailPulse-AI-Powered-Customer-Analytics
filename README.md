## 📌 Project Overview

RetailPulse is an end-to-end Retail Analytics and Business Intelligence solution developed using **Power BI**.

The project transforms raw retail transaction data into interactive dashboards that help businesses monitor sales performance, customer behavior, inventory health, profitability, and seasonal demand trends.

---

# 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Monitor monthly and quarterly revenue trends
- Identify top-performing products
- Perform customer segmentation
- Track inventory risks
- Generate business insights for decision-making
- Build interactive executive dashboards

# 📈 Key Insights

- Strong seasonal sales growth during Q4
- High-value customer segments contribute significantly to revenue
- UK is the leading revenue-generating market
- Top-selling products account for a large share of total sales
- Inventory optimization opportunities identified for fast-moving products
- Profitability varies across international markets
